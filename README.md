## Purpose of Finance Analytics: ##

1) Evaluate financial performance to drive strategic decision-making.
2) Facilitate effective communication with stakeholders and investors.
3) Support budgeting, forecasting, and risk management initiatives.

## Importance of Analyzing Finance Data: ##

1) Establish benchmarks against industry peers and previous periods.
2) Identify trends, anomalies, and key drivers affecting profitability.
3) Provide a foundation for financial planning and decision-making.

## Role of Reports: ##

1) Align financial planning with corporate strategic objectives.
2) Instill confidence in the organization's financial stability.
3) Enable proactive adjustments to improve financial outcomes.

## How to Use? ##

1) Open the respective P&L report PDF to analyze financial performance.
